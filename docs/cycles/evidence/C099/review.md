## Repair boundary

The source commits row/asset-document deletion before its effect closure. That closure dispatches CDN/events, calls Blobstore.delete without checking its return, then handles renditions. Local.delete ignores File.rm's error and returns:ok. Changing only Local.delete cannot provide durable retry after the row is gone. A repair needs atomic durable deletion intent with stored physical ownership, honest pending/failed settlement, restart handling, scope-checked retry and separate remote-event semantics. Do not replay CDN/webhooks blindly or infer deletion intent from an unclaimed file.
