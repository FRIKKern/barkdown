## Prepared proof and subsequent result

Register a test-only after-upload callback, verify its ordering, and block only the owned upload process after checking its media row and original bytes. Begin the HTTP hold, kill that process, and prove admission stays in recovery across a full runtime restart. Read back the row and SHA-256 of every original byte before explicit recovery. Keep an acknowledged control upload and verify it remains unchanged. This is a bounded completed-effect/uncertain-response case; the earlier blob-before-row death window, delete effects, S3 and processing jobs remain separate. Stop on an unverified barrier or any mismatch and retain the disposable runtime and logs.
