The adjacent profile-service completion observer already rejects this same receipt as unconfirmed cleanup and retains its rejection. Main must honor that semantic contract too. `providers/runs.finish()` removes finished runs from the live map and retains at most 200 completed receipts. A repair that checks failure only on the first quit, then snapshots the live map again on retry, can forget the failed run. Retain the owned completion/failure across retries; do not convert missing live membership into proof of cleanup.
