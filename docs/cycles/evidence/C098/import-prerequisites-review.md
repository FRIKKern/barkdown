The [inspected CRT candidate](crt-candidate-01/metadata.json) is still only an installed SDK candidate, version14.50.35719.0 with a valid Microsoft signature; it has not been copied into the app or executed. [Microsoft recommends the redistributable package as a prerequisite and describes version detection, options and servicing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/redistributing-visual-cpp-files?view=msvc-170). [The runtime must be at least as recent as its build tools](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/cpp/windows/latest-supported-vc-redist?view=msvc-170). Imports alone do not establish the build-tool version of every vendor binary. Confirm applicable terms and exact producer/toolchain inputs before choosing the installation payload; no license or distribution approval is inferred from its signature.
